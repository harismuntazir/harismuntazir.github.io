Privacy Policy

App: Ghoasted (hm.bytefy.ghoasted)

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how ByteFy ("we", "our", or "us") handles user privacy for the application Ghoasted ("Application", "Service"). Ghoasted is designed as a privacy-first digital discipline and focus utility. We prioritize your privacy and strictly minimize data collection.

2. Google User Data & Google Drive AppData Scope

Ghoasted provides an optional cloud backup and restore feature ("Cloud Vault") utilizing Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API.

Google Drive API Access: drive.appdata

Ghoasted specifically and exclusively requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope.

  • Isolated Storage: This scope only allows Ghoasted to store and access configuration backup files in a hidden, application-specific directory (Application Data folder).
  • No Access to Personal Files: Ghoasted cannot view, read, modify, search, or delete any of your personal Google Drive documents, photos, spreadsheets, or folders.
  • Data Transferred: Only your self-configured app lock rules, schedules, reward ratios, and group structures are saved in the backup file.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: Your Google user data, authentication tokens, and backup files are never transmitted to any third-party servers, analytics trackers, or external databases.

3. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

To enforce lockdowns and help users maintain focus, Ghoasted requires specific local Android permissions. All rule checks occur entirely on your local device:

Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Used solely to detect when a user launches or views a locked application in order to display the focus overlay or redirect them to a productive app. Ghoasted does not record keystrokes, read personal messages, or monitor screen contents.

Device Administrator (BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN)

Used exclusively to protect active lockdowns and prevent premature uninstallation or bypassing while the Self-Discipline shield is active.

Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)

Used on-device only for the "Location Lock" feature to determine whether the device is within or outside a user-selected geofence boundary. Location coordinates are never uploaded, logged, or shared.

Notification Listener Service (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE)

Used to automatically silence and dismiss incoming notifications from apps that are currently in an active lockdown period, preventing unwanted distractions. Notification contents are never stored or read.

Usage Stats (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

Used to track daily app screen time and productive reward usage ratios locally on the device.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

All user data is stored strictly on your local device. If you use Google Drive Cloud Backup, you can delete your backup file at any time directly through Google Drive settings (Settings > Manage Apps > Ghoasted > Delete hidden app data) or by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

Our Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
