2. Google User Data & Google Drive AppData Scope (Ghoasted App)

For users of Ghoasted who choose to enable the optional Cloud Vault feature, the application interfaces with Google Play Services and the Google Drive REST API.

Exclusive Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

  • Application-Specific Sandbox: This scope isolates your backup data within a hidden, dedicated application folder in your Google Drive storage that only Ghoasted can access.
  • No Access to Personal Drive Files: The application does not and cannot access, view, read, index, modify, or delete any of your personal Google Drive documents, photos, sheets, or existing files.
  • Data Stored in Backup: Only your self-configured app lock rules, schedules, reward ratios, and group structures are serialized into the JSON backup package.
  • Zero External Sharing: Your Google user data, authentication tokens, and backup files are never transmitted to ByteFy servers or any third parties.

3. Android Permissions & Local Operation

Our applications operate strictly on-device. The following Android permissions are used strictly to deliver local functionality:

Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Used exclusively by Ghoasted to detect when a locked application is launched to present focus overlays or trigger redirects. It does not monitor keystrokes or inspect personal user content.

Device Administrator (BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN)

Used solely to safeguard active focus sessions from premature uninstallation while the Self-Discipline shield is active.

Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)

Used on-device only for geofencing enforcement (e.g. blocking apps inside/outside specific zones). Coordinates are never stored or transmitted.

Notification Listener (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE)

Used to automatically silence and dismiss incoming notifications from apps that are currently in an active lockdown state.

4. Offline Calculations & Data Retention

For calculation and utility apps (Logs Calculator, PurchLog, Omnivert, In Out), all calculation histories, logs, expense receipts, and currency conversions are stored entirely offline on the user's device in local databases (SQLite/Room) and are erased when the app is cleared or uninstalled.
